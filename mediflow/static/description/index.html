Run an entire clinic from one install

MEDIFLOW unifies clinical care, diagnostics and the revenue cycle for multi-specialty clinics, hospital outpatient departments and reference laboratories. It replaces a dozen disconnected point solutions with a single, governed Odoo application — so your data, billing and compliance finally live in one place.

EMR-lite

Patients, practitioners, encounters, problems, allergies, vitals, immunizations, consent and clinical documents — company-scoped and PHI-audited.

Scheduling

Resource-based, conflict-free booking with a database-level double-booking guard and a live calendar for every practitioner and room.

Laboratory & Diagnostics

LOINC catalog, panels, orders, specimen accessioning and verified, released results with hard immutability and critical-value alerting.

Pharmacy

Prescriptions with allergy and interaction checks, FEFO expiry-aware dispensing on real Odoo Inventory stock moves.

Billing

Charge capture flows from clinical events straight into Odoo Accounting invoices — fully multi-currency and multi-company aware.

Insurance & Claims

Payers, coverage, pre-authorization and the full claim lifecycle with automatic patient-responsibility recomputation.

Patient Portal

Self-service appointments, consent-gated lab results and online invoice payment — branded and secure.

Queue Management

Per-service-point tokens with a live waiting board to keep the floor moving and patients informed.

AI Assist · safety-first

Explainable no-show prediction, triage acuity suggestions and human-reviewed summaries. Predictions run offline; narratives fall back to deterministic templates.

FHIR R4 API

Read-oriented, consent-gated Patient, Encounter, Observation, DiagnosticReport, MedicationRequest and Coverage resources.

Analytics

Operational, clinical and revenue dashboards computed in PostgreSQL views and isolated per company.

Event-driven Integrations

HMAC-signed webhooks, durable domain events, Stripe payment links, Twilio SMS/WhatsApp reminders, telehealth rooms and an authenticated HL7 inbound endpoint.

Multi-currency & global by design

Every monetary field is currency-aware and multi-currency is switched on out of the box. One click applies a Region Profile that localizes language, currency, timezone and the governing privacy framework.

Security & compliance

Every clinical record is company-scoped, PHI-audited with an append-only access & change log, and governed by a guard-protected state machine. National identifiers and clinical exports are consent-gated. Role-based access spans reception, nursing, physicians, lab tech/verifier, pharmacist, cashier, insurance, inventory, compliance and clinic manager.

Built on pure Odoo core

Extends base, mail, contacts, calendar, account, stock, portal, website and bus. No mandatory external Python dependencies — deploys on Odoo.sh and on-premise alike. Optional Stripe and Twilio connectors activate only when configured.
