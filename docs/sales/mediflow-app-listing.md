## Why MEDIFLOW

Running a modern clinic or diagnostic network means stitching together a dozen
disconnected tools — scheduling here, lab results there, billing somewhere else,
and compliance everywhere. **MEDIFLOW unifies the entire patient journey on one
secure, auditable platform**, from the first appointment to the final paid claim.

Built by **SA Systems** on the proven Odoo 18 framework, MEDIFLOW is engineered
for scale — **100+ clinics and millions of records** — without sacrificing the
speed clinicians need at the point of care.

> **The result:** fewer no-shows, faster lab turnaround, cleaner claims, happier
> patients, and a compliance posture you can prove on demand.

---

## What you get

### 🩺 Complete clinical core (EMR)
Patients, encounters, problems, allergies, vitals, immunizations and consent —
a longitudinal record that projects cleanly to **FHIR R4** for interoperability.

### 📅 Smart scheduling & queues
Concurrency-safe booking, walk-in queues, live waiting-room boards, and
**AI no-show prediction** that flags at-risk appointments before they happen.

### 🔬 Diagnostics & lab
Orders, specimens, panels and **immutable, verifier-gated results** with LOINC
coding, critical-value flags and a full amendment audit trail.

### 💊 Pharmacy & inventory
Allergy and duplicate-therapy checks, **FEFO dispensing**, lot/expiry tracking
and near-expiry alerts.

### 💳 Billing, insurance & claims
Charge master, patient-responsibility invoicing, payer coverage, pre-auth and
**end-to-end claims** (submit → adjudicate → pay/deny → appeal).

### 🤖 AI that adds real value — safely
- **No-show risk** scoring (transparent, explainable, fully offline)
- **Smart triage** acuity suggestions from vitals and chief complaint
- **AI-drafted clinical summaries** — always human-reviewed, never auto-signed
- **Plain-language result explanations** for patients and clinicians
- Pluggable LLM provider (OpenAI, Azure, Anthropic, or **local/offline Ollama**)
- **PHI redaction + consent gating + full audit** on every AI call

### 🌍 Global from day one
One-click **region profiles** for the US, EU, UK, KSA, UAE, India, Singapore,
Australia, Canada and Brazil — each bundling language, currency, timezone and the
right regulatory framework (**HIPAA, GDPR, PDPA, LGPD, PIPEDA, PDPL, DPDP**) with
the correct retention and breach-notification rules.

### 🔌 Connected to your ecosystem
Stripe payment links, Twilio **SMS / WhatsApp** reminders, one-click
**telehealth** video rooms, and an authenticated **HL7 v2 / FHIR inbound** endpoint
for lab analyzers and HIS systems — all consent-aware and fully logged.

### 🔐 Compliance built into the foundation
Append-only audit logs, PHI access auditing, per-clinic data isolation,
role-based security across 14 healthcare roles, durable event sourcing and
signed webhooks. **Security isn't a feature here — it's the architecture.**

### 🧑‍💻 Patient portal
Self-service appointments, **consent-gated** lab results, and **online bill pay** —
branded, accessible, and mobile-friendly.
